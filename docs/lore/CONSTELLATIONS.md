# Constellation Lore

Starter lore for the first charted constellations. A recorder attaches lore with `annotate_constellation`, at most 512 bytes. Each entry below fits that limit.

## The Lantern (anchor: region 1)
Lit, the story goes, by the first nomads to cross the Veil, who left a fire burning on every world they passed so the ones behind could follow. Navigators still steer by it when the nebula thickens.

## The Drifting Anchor (anchor: region 7)
Seven stars in the shape of a ship's anchor, slowly pulling apart. Old haulers say it marks the spot where the first freighter fleet broke formation and scattered into the deep.

## The Serpent's Coil (anchor: region 12)
A long, winding chain of pale stars. Wormhole runners treat it as a warning: the regions under the Coil are where the unstable passages open most often.

## The Twin Wardens (anchor: region 3)
Two bright stars that never seem to drift. Guilds swear oaths beneath them, and a house that hangs its banner under the Wardens is said never to fall.

## The Broken Crown (anchor: region 19)
Five stars of a crown with one missing. Collectors believe the lost star is still out there, and that whoever charts it will complete the oldest map in the nebula.
